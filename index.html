Everything the treasurer needs

Built for how our church actually handles money: two currencies, designated gifts, and a team that needs to see the same numbers.

USD and KHR, side by side

Record each amount in the currency it was given or spent. Nothing is converted; totals are shown per currency.

Income and expenses

Tithes, offerings, donations and every expense, with category, payment method, fund and a photo of the receipt.

Designated funds

Building, mission and general funds each keep their own balance, so designated gifts are spent as intended.

Dashboard and reports

Monthly income, expenses and net, a six-month trend with a USD / KHR switch, and budget progress at a glance.

Code dictionary

Every category has a code and a "when to use it" tip, like I-01 Tithes or E-14 Electricity, so entries stay consistent.

Safe and accountable

Each person sees only their church's data, by role. Every change is kept in an audit log, and transactions are reversed, never deleted.

Every transaction gets a number

Numbers are assigned automatically when you save, so anyone can find, check or correct a record by quoting it.

How to start

Takes about a minute the first time.

Open the app

Click Launch app on this page. Add it to your phone's home screen for quick access.

Sign in with Google

Use the Google account the church admin added. New here? Ask the treasurer or an admin for access.

Record and review

Use Record income or Record expense, pick a category from the code list, and check the dashboard.

Ready to open the books?

The app runs securely on GitHub Pages and Supabase. Your sign-in decides what you can see and change.
